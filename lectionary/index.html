Revised Common Lectionary · 2026

Weekly Lectionary

A page for each Sunday from Christ the King through Christmas Eve and Christmas Day. Each one has the readings, the Gospel in the World English Bible, a short study note, questions for a small group, and a prayer.

November 22 is still Year A, the last Sunday of that cycle. On November 29, Advent begins and Year B begins with it. The citations were checked against the Vanderbilt Divinity Library lectionary for 2025–26 and 2026–27. Wherever Scripture is quoted on these pages, it is the World English Bible, which is in the public domain.

The weeks

  • Christ the King (Reign of Christ)Ezekiel 34 and Matthew 25:31–46. The shepherd who judges, and the King who is met among the least.November 22
  • First Sunday of AdventIsaiah 64 and Mark 13:24–37. Hope is not a mood. It is a household that stays awake.November 29
  • Second Sunday of AdventIsaiah 40 and Mark 1:1–8. A level highway, and a baptism that prepares the way.December 6
  • Third Sunday of AdventIsaiah 61 and John 1:6–8, 19–28. Rejoice: among you stands one whom you do not know.December 13
  • Fourth Sunday of Advent2 Samuel 7 and Luke 1:26–38. A kingdom with no end, and a servant of the Lord in Nazareth.December 20
  • Christmas Eve and Christmas Day, Proper IIsaiah 9 and Luke 2:1–14 (15–20). Good news of great joy, and a Savior who is Christ the Lord.December 24–25
